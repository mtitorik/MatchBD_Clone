COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about safety, privacy, and finding your match on MatchBD.

How does MatchBD protect my personal biodata and phone number? +

Unlike WhatsApp groups or traditional ghataks who forward biodatas openly, your personal contact details, residential address, and phone numbers are never made public on MatchBD. Information is only shared when you explicitly approve a mutual match.

How are profiles verified on MatchBD? +

All members must submit a government-issued National ID card (NID) or Passport along with a live selfie verification. Our in-house Dhaka compliance team reviews credentials to ensure authentic profiles and eliminate impersonators.

Can Non-Resident Bangladeshis (Probashis) use MatchBD? +

Yes! MatchBD supports Bangladeshi diaspora members across the USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Europe, and the Middle East. You can filter candidates by current country of residence, immigration status, or intent to relocate.

Can family members create a profile on behalf of a son, daughter, or sibling? +

Yes, MatchBD accommodates family-assisted matchmaking. When registering, parents or guardians can specify that the profile is managed on behalf of their child, allowing collaborative reviews while respecting privacy.

What payment methods are supported in Bangladesh? +

We accept all major local payment methods via SSLCommerz including bKash, Nagad, Rocket, Upay, local bank debit/credit cards (Visa, Mastercard), as well as international cards for Probashis.

What if I encounter an inappropriate user or scammer? +

MatchBD has zero tolerance for harassment or fraudulent behavior. You can immediately block and report any profile with a single tap. Our safety team acts on reports within 2 hours and takes strict action including permanent device bans.
